\begin{solapartat}
Els electrons son frenats pel camp elèctric, transformant la seva energia cinètica en energia potencial elèctrica: \punts{0,2}
\[ \Delta E_c = \Delta E_p \Rightarrow \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = |\mathrm{e}|\Delta V\ \text{\punts{0,2}} \]
\[ v = \sqrt{\frac{2|\mathrm{e}|\Delta V}{m}} \Rightarrow \]
\[ \left\{\begin{aligned}
v_1 &= \sqrt{\tfrac{2\times 1,60\times 10^{-19}\times 4,01}{9,11\times 10^{-31}}} = 1,19\times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}\ \text{\punts{0,3}}\\
v_2 &= \sqrt{\tfrac{2\times 1,60\times 10^{-19}\times 8,15}{9,11\times 10^{-31}}} = 1,69\times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}\ \text{\punts{0,3}}
\end{aligned}\right. \]
\end{solapartat}

\begin{solapartat}
El balanç energètic en l'efecte fotoelèctric és:
\[ E = \frac{hc}{\lambda} = W_0 + E_c\ \text{\punts{0,1}} = W_0 + |\mathrm{e}|\Delta V\ \text{\punts{0,1}} \]

Per tant
\[ \left.\begin{aligned}
\tfrac{hc}{\lambda_1} &= W_0 + |\mathrm{e}|\Delta V_1\\
\tfrac{hc}{\lambda_2} &= W_0 + |\mathrm{e}|\Delta V_2
\end{aligned}\right\} \Rightarrow \text{\punts{0,1}} h\left\{\frac{c}{\lambda_1} - \frac{c}{\lambda_2}\right\} = |\mathrm{e}|\left\{\Delta V_1 - \Delta V_2\right\}\ \text{\punts{0,1}} \Rightarrow \]
\[ h = \frac{|\mathrm{e}|}{c}\,\frac{\Delta V_1 - \Delta V_2}{\frac{1}{\lambda_1} - \frac{1}{\lambda_2}} = 6,62\times 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}\ \text{\punts{0,3}} \]

Per altre banda, substituint en una les equacions anteriors:
\[ W_0 = \frac{hc}{\lambda_1} - |\mathrm{e}|\Delta V_1 = 6,82\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}\ \text{\punts{0,3}} \]
\end{solapartat}
